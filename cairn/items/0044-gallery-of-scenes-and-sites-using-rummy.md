## Problem

Nothing sells a visual tool like a wall of things made with it.

## Proposal

A gallery page: every built-in scene and preset as a live tile (one shared
WebGL context, tiles rendered in turn, off-screen tiles paused), community
scenes submitted as playground links via PR, and a "made with rummy" section
for real sites. The contact sheet (`/shots.html`) is the seed of this.

## Cost

Demo site only.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All scenes and presets live on one page at 60 fps on a laptop
- [ ] Submission path documented
